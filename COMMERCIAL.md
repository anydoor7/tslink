## Who can use it without a commercial agreement?

| Use | Community permission |
|---|---|
| Personal study, hobby projects, home services; no organizational business or operational benefit | Free under the additional personal-use permission, regardless of the size of an employer that does not benefit from the use |
| An individual's own business or a small organization | Free while both company-size tests below are satisfied |
| An employer, client, or controlled group that does not satisfy either test | Obtain a separate written commercial license before use outside the community permissions |
| Material already obtained under Apache 2.0, or third-party components | Existing respective licenses continue to govern; the new community terms do not revoke them |

For organizational use, **both** tests must be satisfied:

1. Fewer than **100 total individuals** working as employees and independent contractors.
2. Less than **USD 1,000,000 in 2019 dollars**, adjusted for inflation as specified
   in the PolyForm license, in total revenue in the **prior tax year**.

Exactly 100 people or revenue equal to the adjusted threshold does not
satisfy the relevant test. Count the organization and entities controlling,
controlled by, or under common control with it, as defined in the license.
Count people, including independent contractors, rather than TSLink seats,
servers, installations, or a single team's headcount. An employee using
TSLink for work must assess the employer; calling the operator an individual
or using a personal laptop does not change the beneficiary.

The income threshold uses the US Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI-U,
US city average, all items, not seasonally adjusted, 1982–1984=100
(series CUUR0000SA0). The license does not specify a sampling month.
As an **annual-average illustration for calendar tax year 2025**, the official
2019 and 2025 annual indexes are 255.657 and 321.943:
`1,000,000 × 321.943 / 255.657 ≈ USD 1,259,277.08`.
This illustration is not a fixed new dollar threshold or an amendment to
the license. For a non-calendar tax year, currency conversion, a new entity
without a prior tax year, or a borderline calculation, we recommend requesting written clarification.
This recommendation adds no notification or approval condition to an existing
community grant.

Nonprofit, educational, and government organizations receive no automatic
size exemption in this license. Personal student use can qualify under the
personal-use permission; institutional operation is assessed as organizational
use. A contractor serving a client must consider the actual beneficiary and
the license's company/control definition; complex shared-service arrangements
can be clarified in writing. This is guidance, not an additional condition
on rights already granted by the license.

## Growth and commercial cooperation

Eligible community users do not need to register, notify us, or pay a fee.
If a planned use is outside the community permissions, obtain a separate
written license first. If growth or acquisition will end eligibility, we recommend contacting
us before the change to arrange any needed license; advance notification is
not an additional condition on currently eligible community use. There is no automatic 90-day growth allowance or
enterprise evaluation license. The PolyForm license's first-notice violation
remedy is governed by its own text and is not a general trial period.

Commercial terms are negotiated. The written agreement can define covered
entities, deployment scope, duration, fees if any, support, and any service
commitments. Contacting us alone is neither permission to continue otherwise
unlicensed use nor agreement to an unspecified charge. Cooperation can include
paid licensing, support, or another expressly agreed arrangement.

Commercial contact: **PUBLIC_CONTACT_PENDING**.
Include the organization and controlled group, approximate employee/contractor
count, prior-tax-year revenue band, intended use, and support needs. Send
credentials, customer data, and confidential financial records only through
an appropriate private channel agreed for that purpose; they are not needed
for an initial inquiry.

## Redistribution, old versions, and dependencies

This policy adds no blanket prohibition on resale, hosting, competition,
or redistribution beyond the actual license. The PolyForm Distribution
License grants distribution rights; recipients' use is assessed under their
own applicable permissions. Comply with the license's Notices clause (terms or their URL and Required
Notice lines), and with third-party notice obligations. Do not claim every large distributor must buy a license solely to
redistribute unmodified copies. Operating hosted services for an organization's
business benefit remains subject to its applicable use permissions and size tests.

Earlier Apache-licensed TSLink code remains available under its existing
rights, including commercial use and downstream forks. The historical boundary
is the pre-change tree at `cf5ac1f16d8712c17675505ccf3e1ed906aab17b`;
this change does not make old Apache code exclusive. New value in later
community-licensed material is what can support future commercial licensing.
Third-party software keeps its original license and attribution requirements.
